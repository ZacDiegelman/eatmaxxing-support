Support

Thanks for using DailyBread. Here is help with common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact

To report a problem, ask a question or send feedback, open a support request (a free GitHub account is required). We read every request.

Report a nutrition error

If a number in DailyBread doesn’t match what the restaurant publishes, please send a nutrition correction with:

  • the restaurant and menu item (and size or variant),
  • which value looks wrong, and
  • where the restaurant publishes the correct value (a link or a photo of the guide), if you have it.

We correct data only from the restaurant’s own published information. We don’t change values based on estimates.

Common questions

Where does the nutrition come from?

From nutrition information each restaurant publishes, such as its official nutrition guide or website. Every restaurant page in the app has a Nutrition source button that shows the source and links to it.

Why does a value show “—”?

The restaurant didn’t publish that nutrient, or published it in a way that couldn’t be read reliably. DailyBread never fills it in with a guess or a zero. When you filter by that nutrient, those items are hidden, and the app tells you how many and lets you show them.

Why does a value show “<1”?

The restaurant lists it as less than that amount. It’s shown exactly that way, and it isn’t used for sorting or filtering.

Why is a nutrient marked as conflicting?

Sometimes a restaurant’s published number contradicts its own totals (for example, more saturated fat than total fat). DailyBread hides that one value rather than pick a side.

Why does a nearby restaurant say “Nutrition coming soon” or “not available yet”?

DailyBread lists every restaurant near you, but only shows menu nutrition after the restaurant’s own published numbers pass its checks. Until then the page says so — DailyBread never estimates nutrition to fill the gap. Items with questionable published values are held back until they can be confirmed.

Why does DailyBread ask for my location?

To show restaurants around you. It explains this before asking, uses “While Using the App” access only, and compares your location against a restaurant-location database stored inside the app — your location never leaves your iPhone and isn’t stored. If you say no, enter a city or ZIP code on Home instead; everything else works. You can change this any time in the iPhone Settings app under DailyBread → Location.

A restaurant location is wrong or missing

Store locations come from restaurants’ own store locators (via AllThePlaces) and OpenStreetMap, and are updated with app releases, so a recently opened or closed store may be out of date. Tell us below and we’ll check it.

How do I remove saved items or history?

Tap the bookmark on a saved item to remove it. To clear recently viewed restaurants, go to Settings → Clear recently viewed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Allergies and medical needs

Restaurants change recipes and portions, and nutrition varies by location and preparation. If you have an allergy, a medical condition or strict dietary needs, confirm with the restaurant. DailyBread does not provide medical advice. See Terms & Nutrition Information.
