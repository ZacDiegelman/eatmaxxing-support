Support

Thanks for using EatMaxxing. Here is help with common questions, and how to reach us.

Contact

To report a problem, ask a question or send feedback, open a support request (a free GitHub account is required). We read every request.

Report a nutrition error

If a number in EatMaxxing doesn’t match what the restaurant publishes, please send a nutrition correction with:

  • the restaurant and menu item (and size or variant),
  • which value looks wrong, and
  • where the restaurant publishes the correct value (a link or a photo of the guide), if you have it.

We correct data only from the restaurant’s own published information. We don’t change values based on estimates.

Common questions

Where does the nutrition come from?

From nutrition information each restaurant publishes, such as its official nutrition guide or website. Every restaurant page in the app has a Nutrition source button that shows the source and links to it.

Why does a value show “—”?

The restaurant didn’t publish that nutrient, or published it in a way that couldn’t be read reliably. EatMaxxing never fills it in with a guess or a zero. When you filter by that nutrient, those items are hidden, and the app tells you how many and lets you show them.

Why does a value show “<1”?

The restaurant lists it as less than that amount. It’s shown exactly that way, and it isn’t used for sorting or filtering.

Why is a nutrient marked as conflicting?

Sometimes a restaurant’s published number contradicts its own totals (for example, more saturated fat than total fat). EatMaxxing hides that one value rather than pick a side.

Why are some items or restaurants missing?

EatMaxxing only shows restaurants and items whose official nutrition passed its checks. Items with questionable published values are held back until they can be confirmed.

Why does Nearby ask for my location?

Only to find restaurants around you, and only after you tap Use my location. Your location isn’t stored. If you say no, everything else in the app still works. You can change this any time in the iPhone Settings app under EatMaxxing → Location.

How do I remove saved items or history?

Tap the bookmark on a saved item to remove it. To clear recently viewed restaurants, go to Settings → Clear recently viewed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Allergies and medical needs

Restaurants change recipes and portions, and nutrition varies by location and preparation. If you have an allergy, a medical condition or strict dietary needs, confirm with the restaurant. EatMaxxing does not provide medical advice. See Terms & Nutrition Information.
