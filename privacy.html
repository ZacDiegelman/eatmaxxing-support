Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 (updated for location-first browsing)

EatMaxxing is designed to work without collecting personal information. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t collect, sell or share personal data.
  • Saved items, recently viewed restaurants, your goals and your search radius stay on your iPhone.
  • With your permission, your location is used on your iPhone to find nearby restaurants. It is never sent to us, stored or shared.

Information stored on your iPhone

EatMaxxing keeps a few small files in the app’s storage on your device: the menu items you save, the restaurants you recently viewed, the goals and nutrient priorities you choose during setup, your search radius, and — only if you type one — the city, ZIP code or address you chose instead of your current location. None of this is sent to us or anyone else. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your device, according to your iPhone settings. You can change or clear each item in the app (Saved, Settings), or delete the app to remove all of it.

Location

EatMaxxing shows restaurants near you. It explains why before asking, asks only for “While Using the App” access, and never uses background location. When you allow it, your iPhone’s location is compared, on your iPhone, against a database of restaurant locations that is built into the app. Your location is not sent to us or to any other service, is not stored, and is not used for advertising or tracking. If you show your location on the map, Apple Maps (MapKit) draws the map under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

You don’t have to share your location. You can type a city, ZIP code or address instead. That text is turned into map coordinates by Apple’s geocoding service (part of iOS, covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy); EatMaxxing does not receive it. The place you choose is remembered on your iPhone so you don’t have to type it again. You can change location access at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Restaurant locations and logos

Restaurant store locations are bundled with the app from AllThePlaces (data gathered from restaurants’ own store locators, CC0) and OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License). Restaurant logos are bundled with the app from Wikimedia Commons or the restaurant’s own website and are used only to identify each restaurant. Looking up nearby restaurants and showing logos does not make network requests.

Nutrition data

Restaurant menus and nutrition are built into the app. Browsing, searching, filtering, sorting and saving happen entirely on your device and don’t send your searches or choices anywhere. EatMaxxing has no server of its own and contains no AI chatbot.

Links to other sites and apps

If you open a restaurant’s official nutrition source, or ask for directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, that website or app handles your visit under its own privacy policy. For directions, EatMaxxing passes only the restaurant’s location to the maps app you choose.

Children

EatMaxxing is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app’s practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact EatMaxxing support.
