Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

EatMaxxing is designed to work without collecting personal information. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • We don’t collect, sell or share personal data.
  • Saved items and recently viewed restaurants stay on your iPhone.
  • Location is used only when you ask to see nearby restaurants, and it isn’t stored.

Information stored on your iPhone

EatMaxxing keeps two small lists in the app’s storage on your device: the menu items you save and the restaurants you recently viewed. They are not sent to us or anyone else. They may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your device, according to your iPhone settings. You can remove saved items in the Saved tab, clear recently viewed restaurants in Settings, or delete the app to remove both.

Location

The Nearby tab can show restaurants around you. It asks for permission only after you tap Use my location, and it uses “While Using the App” access only. Your location is used on your iPhone to search Apple Maps (Apple’s MapKit service) for nearby restaurant locations and to show them on a map. EatMaxxing does not store your location or send it to us or to any other service. Apple’s handling of Maps requests is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. If you don’t allow location access, all other features keep working, and you can change your choice at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Nutrition data

Restaurant menus and nutrition are built into the app. Browsing, searching, filtering, sorting and saving happen entirely on your device and don’t send your searches or choices anywhere.

Links to other sites and apps

If you open a restaurant’s official nutrition source, or ask for directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze, that website or app handles your visit under its own privacy policy. For directions, EatMaxxing passes only the restaurant’s location to the maps app you choose.

Children

EatMaxxing is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app’s practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact EatMaxxing support.
