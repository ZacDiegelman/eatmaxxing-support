Eat out. Stay on track.

EatMaxxing is an iPhone app that shows the restaurants around you and helps you find what fits your calories, protein and macros. It uses nutrition information that restaurants publish themselves, shows where every number came from, and never fills a missing value with a guess or a zero.

What you can do

  • See restaurants near you (or near any city or ZIP code), nearest first, within 5–100 miles.
  • Open a menu with official nutrition, sorted for your goal.
  • Sort by most protein, protein per calorie, fewest calories, carbs, fat or sodium.
  • Filter with your own calorie and macro ranges, or quick goals like High protein or Cutting.
  • Save go-to items on your iPhone and get directions to the nearest location.

EatMaxxing lists 800+ U.S. restaurant chains. Official nutrition is available for a growing selection of them whose published information could be verified; the app always shows which ones.

EatMaxxing is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any restaurant shown in the app. Restaurant names and logos are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify each restaurant.
