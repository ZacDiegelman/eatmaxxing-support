What can I order that fits my macros?

EatMaxxing is an iPhone app for browsing restaurant menus by calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and sodium. It uses nutrition information that restaurants publish themselves, shows where every number came from, and never fills a missing value with a guess or a zero.

What you can do

  • Browse and search restaurant menus with official nutrition.
  • Sort by most protein, protein per calorie, fewest calories, carbs, fat or sodium.
  • Filter with your own calorie and macro ranges, or quick goals like High protein or Cutting.
  • Save go-to items on your iPhone and find restaurants near you.

EatMaxxing covers a selection of U.S. restaurant chains whose official nutrition information could be verified. Coverage grows over time.

EatMaxxing is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any restaurant shown in the app. Restaurant names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify their menus.
